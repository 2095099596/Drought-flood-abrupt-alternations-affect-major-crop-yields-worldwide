# Drought-flood-abrupt-alternations-affect-major-crop-yields-worldwide

## Main: Crop Yield Residual Calculation

**Function**

Calculates gridded crop yield residuals under different LDFAI thresholds across three phenological stages (planting, growing, and harvesting). Supports multiple detrending methods and provides the fundamental dataset for subsequent figures and analyses.

**Input**
- Crop yield data (`merged_yield_data.nc`)
- Crop phenology data (`*.crop.calendar.fill.nc`)
- Monthly LDFAI data (`.nc`)

**Output**
- Gridded mean yield residuals for three phenological stages under different LDFAI thresholds (`.nc`)
- Best-fit trend model maps (`.nc`)
- Results from linear (`linear_bic`), quadratic (`quadratic_bic`), and multi-model (`multimodel_aic`) detrending methods

## Figure1a

### Figure1a_1

#### 1. Correlation Between Drought–Flood Abrupt Alternation Events and Crop Yield Anomalies

**Function**

Analyzes the relationship between drought–flood abrupt alternation events (LDFAI) of varying intensity and detrended crop yield residuals across crop phenological stages, and compares SPI-LDFAI with SPEI-LDFAI.

**Input**
- Crop yield data (`merged_yield_data.nc`)
- Crop phenology data (`Soybeans.crop.calendar.fill.nc`)
- Monthly SPI-LDFAI and SPEI-LDFAI data (`.nc`)

**Output** (generated separately for |LDFAI| > 4–9)
- `*_correlation_summary.csv`: Correlations, significance, and sample sizes
- `*_spi_spei_compare.csv`: SPI vs. SPEI comparison
- `*_pairs.csv`: Paired LDFAI and yield residual samples
- `*_count_check.csv`: SPI/SPEI sample count consistency check

### Figure1a_2

#### 2. Visualization of SPI-LDFAI and SPEI-LDFAI Correlation Differences

**Function**

Visualizes differences in the absolute correlations between SPI-LDFAI and SPEI-LDFAI with crop yield anomalies across phenological stages for four major crops (maize, rice, soybeans, and wheat), using heatmaps.

**Input**
- SPI–SPEI correlation comparison results (`event_ldfai_abs_gt4_spi_spei_compare.csv`) for four crops, generated by `Figure1a_1`

**Output**
- Individual crop correlation comparison heatmaps (`.png`, `.pdf`)
- Combined 2 × 2 heatmap for four crops using the linear trend model (`linear_bic`) and Raw Max/Raw Min LDFAI features (`.png`, `.pdf`)
